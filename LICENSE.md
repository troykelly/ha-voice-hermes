- `firmware/**/*.cpp` and `firmware/**/*.h`: **GPL-3.0-only**.
- firmware Python/YAML, documentation, and repository metadata: **MIT**.
- `gateway/`: **MIT**, as declared in `gateway/Cargo.toml`.
